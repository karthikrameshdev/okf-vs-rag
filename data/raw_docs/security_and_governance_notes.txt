Enterprise Security Policies and Zero-Trust Compliance Overview

Policy Document 2026-SEC-01: Zero-Trust Network Architecture
All workloads operating within FinCloud are subject to zero-trust network verification. Perimeter firewalls are no longer considered adequate boundaries. Every microservice pod must present SPIFFE/SPIRE X.509 certificates for mutual TLS. Supported cipher suites are restricted to TLS 1.3 with AES-256-GCM and ChaCha20-Poly1305. Services subject to this standard include Payment Gateway V2, User Authentication Service, Order Service, and Notification Service.

Policy Document 2026-SEC-02: PCI-DSS v4.0 Cardholder Data Environment Standards
Cardholder Data Environment (CDE) is restricted to strictly necessary components: Payment Gateway V2 and Vault Secrets Engine. Primary Account Numbers (PAN) must never be written to persistent storage in unencrypted form. Dynamic tokenization via AES-256-GCM is enforced through the Vault Secrets Engine. Audit logs must be retained for at least 365 days.
